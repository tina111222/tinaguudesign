App Bill Installment Feature Optimization

Improving Clarity in a Financial Application Flow

UI/UX Design Design System Accessibility

I refined the bill installment interface to make key information easier to read, clarify application progress, and present important financial disclosures in a more structured way.

  • My Role: UI/UX Designer
  • Goal: Improve readability, guide users through the application flow, and make required disclosures easier to review.
Experience the Prototype

1. Design System Foundations & Visual Optimization

To ensure strict alignment with enterprise brand guidelines, I redefined the app's color and typography tokens. Text sizes, numerical data, and padding were systematically scaled up to maximize readability. The overall palette was refined for a softer, more cohesive visual harmony.

  • Typography Hierarchy: Established clear, accessible type scales.
  • Systematic Color Palette: Implemented consistent tokens including Primary Red, soft Alert Pink, and balanced grays to unify the visual language.

2. Guiding Users Through Each Step

I redesigned the application progress indicator to make the current stage and completed steps easier to identify.

  • Active Step: Red concentric circles highlight the current stage.
  • Completed Step: A red checkmark indicates a completed stage.
  • Upcoming Steps: Gray styling distinguishes steps that have not yet been reached.

3. Information Hierarchy & Error Prevention

Highlighting Important Deadlines
I gave the application deadline greater visual emphasis to help users identify time-sensitive information.

Making Important Notices Easier to Recognize
I used a soft pink background for important notices and system restrictions, drawing attention to critical information without making the interface feel overly aggressive.

Progressive Disclosure for Compliance Information

To balance strict regulatory compliance with a clean mobile UI, I implemented a progressive disclosure pattern for the lengthy legal terms:

Accordion-Driven Compliance: Dense financial disclosures are neatly collapsed by default to minimize initial cognitive overload. Users are required to actively expand and review the content before the consent checkbox is unlocked, ensuring legal safety without compromising the app's streamlined user flow.
