Rakuten Card Campaign Page:
UI/UX Redesign & Front-end Development

UI/UX Design Front-end Development

I redesigned the campaign page to make key offers easier to discover, simplify content browsing, and create space for new business campaigns. I also implemented responsive layouts and interactive carousels using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Swiper.js.

  • My Role: Visual Designer, UI/UX Designer, and Front-end Developer (HTML/CSS/JS).
  • Goal: Improve campaign visibility, simplify content discovery, and support business objectives through a clearer information architecture.
🚀 See It in Action

Core Contributions & Impact

1. Boosting Campaign Visibility: Text Links → Featured Banner Carousel

Legacy Issue

Featured campaigns were displayed as a basic list of text links at the top of the page, limiting their visual impact and making them less prominent.

My Solution

I redesigned the section as a full-width, responsive banner carousel using Swiper.js, giving key campaigns greater visual prominence and making them easier to discover.

2. Simplifying Offer Discovery: Manual Filtering → Card Issuer Hubs

Legacy Issue

Users had to manually filter card types to find relevant offers. At the bottom of the page, card issuer logos served only as links to separate offer pages, requiring extra clicks to discover exclusive campaigns.

My Solution

I removed the manual card-filtering function and reorganized offers into dedicated Visa, Mastercard, and JCB sections, displaying each issuer’s exclusive campaigns directly beneath its logo for easier browsing.

3. Optimizing Page Architecture: Long Campaign Grid → Compact Layout with Dedicated Offer Sections

Legacy Issue

The original page displayed dozens of campaign cards in a long grid, creating excessive scrolling and pushing lower-page content out of view. It also lacked dedicated sections for key business initiatives, including Japan travel perks and the MGM Referral Program.

My Solution

I redesigned the campaign grid to display two rows at a time and integrated a Swiper carousel for additional campaigns. This reduced vertical content density and freed up space for dedicated Japan Travel Perks and MGM Referral Program sections, balancing campaign visibility with efficient use of page space.

Outcome & Technical Implementation

  • A More Structured Campaign Experience
    The redesigned page organizes campaigns into clearer sections, gives featured offers greater visibility, and creates dedicated space for additional business initiatives. The carousel-based layout also provides a more compact way to browse campaigns without displaying every item at once.
  • Maintainable & Responsive Front-end
    I used shared CSS styles and consistent JavaScript breakpoints to manage responsive layouts and Swiper configurations across screen sizes. This reduced redundant media queries and made the front-end code easier to maintain and update.
