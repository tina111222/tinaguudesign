MediaTek Health — Making Health Data Easier to Understand

UI/UX Design Visual Identity

I designed a health diary concept that turns complex biosensing data into clear, easy-to-understand health insights. The goal was to make health monitoring more approachable for younger users and older adults.

  • My Role: UI/UX Designer, Visual Designer
  • Goal: Make health information easier to understand and daily health monitoring easier to use.
Experience the Prototype
01. The Challenge

Making Invisible Health Signals Easier to Understand

Early health risks can be difficult to recognize without regular monitoring. However, biosensing data can be difficult for everyday users to interpret.

Research Insight:
Some young adults may be unaware of their hypertension risk, highlighting an opportunity for more accessible health education.

Design Opportunity:
Make health data easier to understand and encourage users to engage with their daily health.

Inspiration:
Using MediaTek Sensio biosensing technology, I designed MediaTek Health—a diary app that makes these invisible signals impossible to ignore.

02. Research Insights & Design Strategy

Market data from 2022 shows that smart wearable adoption rates in Taiwan are significantly lower among the 20-29 and 50+ age groups compared to the 30-39 segment. My research focused on two potential barriers to everyday health monitoring: the cost of dedicated devices and the complexity of digital interfaces.

Younger Adults — Lowering the Entry Barrier
I explored using existing smartphones as the main access point for health monitoring, reducing the need for an additional dedicated device.
Older Adults — Improving Readability
I designed adjustable text sizes and a simplified diary flow to make the interface easier to read and navigate.

03. Information Architecture & Wireflow

I mapped the core journey from initial setup and measurement to understanding results and sharing health reports with family members.

04. Key Design Decisions

Personalized Typography

Users can adjust text size during profile setup, making the interface more comfortable to read from the start.

Reducing Wait Anxiety

A countdown timer provides clear progress feedback during the 60-second measurement, helping users understand how long they need to wait.

Visualizing Health Concerns

A simplified body diagram helps users connect measurement results with relevant areas of the body, making complex information easier to understand.

Family Health Sharing

Users can share health reports with family members, supporting communication and mutual care.

05. Outcome & Reflection

Outcome
The concept brings measurement, result interpretation, and family sharing into one health diary experience. It explores how biosensing technology can be presented in a way that feels more approachable to everyday users.

Reflection & Next Steps
This project taught me to consider not only how health data is displayed, but also how people understand and act on it.

  • Automated Family Alerts: Replace manual report sharing with automatic alerts to family members when abnormal health readings persist.
  • Smart Reminders: Provide personalized reminders based on health patterns to encourage daily monitoring, rather than relying on one-time checks.
